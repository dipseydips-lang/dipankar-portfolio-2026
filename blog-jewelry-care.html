How to Take Care of Fine Jewelry at Home

D2C / Jewelry · Industry Writing

Fine jewelry can last for generations, but everyday wear exposes pieces to moisture, cosmetics, chemicals, friction and accidental impact. Simple habits can reduce unnecessary damage. Different materials require different care, so the safest approach is to understand what a piece is made from before cleaning or storing it.
