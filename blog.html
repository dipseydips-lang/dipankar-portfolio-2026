Why the archive is smaller

The goal is not to show the largest possible number of URLs. It is to show range, subject understanding and the ability to change the writing approach when the audience changes.

Technical pieces explain systems and trade-offs. Home-service pieces answer practical problems. Product articles help buyers make decisions. B2B pieces focus on business context. The portfolio also keeps personal thought leadership separate from client work so the voice is clear.
