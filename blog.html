Writing archive

Writing that changes with the subject.

SEO strategy, technical explainers, industry writing, ecommerce content and long-form editorial work. The point of this archive is not to show everything I have written. It is to show range: different audiences, different levels of technical depth, different search intent and different ways of making a subject worth reading.

Search-led writingTechnical subjectsIndustry-specific voiceEditorial analysis

The writing mode changes with the brief. Personal essays can take a position and examine where an industry is heading. Technical pieces have to explain systems accurately. Commercial content has to help a reader make a decision. Brand-focused work has to sound like it belongs to the business publishing it.

That distinction matters. A cybersecurity article should not read like an ecommerce product guide, and a homeowner looking for a repair should not have to wade through enterprise jargon. The samples below are arranged to make that range visible.
