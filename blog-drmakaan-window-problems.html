Window Problems Homeowners Should Not Ignore

Client work | Home Repair · Client work

A window can show signs of failure long before it becomes completely unusable. Drafts, condensation between panes, difficult operation, rattling and water intrusion can all point to different repair needs.

Drafts around the frame

Air movement around a closed window can come from worn seals, gaps around the frame or problems with installation. Finding the exact location matters because replacing the glass will not solve a gap around the frame.

Condensation between panes

Moisture trapped between insulated glass panes can indicate a failed seal. The window may still open and close, but its insulating performance can be affected. The appropriate repair depends on the window construction and the condition of the surrounding frame.

Windows that are difficult to open

Stiff operation can result from hardware wear, alignment problems, paint or debris. Forcing the window can create additional damage, especially when the operating mechanism is already under stress.

Water intrusion needs prompt attention

Water around a window should not be treated as a cosmetic issue. The source may be the window, surrounding sealant, flashing or another part of the wall. Early diagnosis can help limit damage to adjacent materials.

Repair versus replacement

Replacement is not the only answer to a window problem. If the frame and major components remain sound, targeted repair can restore operation and performance. A professional assessment can help determine whether repair is practical or whether the assembly has reached the end of its useful life.
