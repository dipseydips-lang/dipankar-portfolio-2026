AI + MCP content systems

Most writers use AI. I build the pipes AI runs through, and write the content AI cites.

Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets AI assistants connect directly to your CMS, SEO tools and data. I design, build and run these systems for content teams, then put a senior writer's judgment on top. The result: agency-scale output with senior-writer quality, engineered to win on Google and to be cited by AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity.

How an engagement works

From audit to autopilot in four steps.

step 01

AEO + GEO

Ranking on Google was the game. Being cited by AI is the new one.

A growing share of your buyers never see page one. They ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews, and read the answer. Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) decide whether that answer mentions you or your competitor. I write content engineered for both games at once.

aeo / 01

Answer-first structure

Direct answers in the first two sentences of every section, question-based H2s and H3s, and self-contained passages an LLM can lift cleanly. AI engines cite sources that answer without making the model work.

aeo / 02

Citable specifics

Concrete numbers, ranges, named steps and defined terms. Vague content gets paraphrased anonymously. Specific content gets cited by name, which is the whole point.

aeo / 03

Schema and entity signals

FAQ, HowTo, Organization and Article schema, consistent entity naming and clean semantic HTML, so both crawlers and language models understand exactly who is saying what.
