Asphalt Driveways: What Affects Their Lifespan?

Home Services · Industry Writing

An asphalt driveway takes a surprising amount of punishment. Cars, rain, temperature changes, standing water and repeated freeze-thaw cycles can all affect its condition. The lifespan of a driveway is not determined by one factor. The base beneath it, drainage, installation quality, traffic and maintenance all play a role. Understanding these factors makes it easier to recognise why two driveways can age very differently.
